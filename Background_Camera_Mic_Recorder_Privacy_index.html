This policy describes how Background Camera/Mic Recorder (the “App”) handles information when you use its background video, audio, camera, advertising, and premium-feature functionality. It applies to the App’s current commercial testing release. Any changes to data processing for a production release will be reflected here.

1. Camera, microphone, and recordings

With your permission, the App accesses your camera and/or microphone to create the video or audio recordings you start. Recording may continue while the phone screen is locked. Android recording indicators and the active foreground-service notification may appear while capture is in progress.

The App saves recorded videos and audio files to local media storage on your device using Android media APIs. The current App code does not upload or transmit the content of these recordings to our servers or to advertising providers. Your recordings remain on your device unless you choose to share them using other apps, or your device or backup settings sync them elsewhere.

2. Information processed through advertisements and consent

The free version contains Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which may process information to request and store advertising/privacy choices and deliver, measure, and protect ads. Depending on your region, configuration, and consent choices, Google or advertising partners may process information including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may indicate approximate location;
  • Device or app identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers where available;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app use, ad impressions or taps;
  • Technical or diagnostic data, including device/app performance and errors.

Ad and consent-related data may be shared with Google and advertising partners acting in accordance with their policies and applicable law. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. The current testing build uses test ads; a later production build may use live ads.

Where supported, you can review advertising privacy choices in the App. You can also manage advertising and application permissions through Android device settings.

3. Premium features and Google Play purchases

The App uses Google Play Billing for product information, purchases, and restoration of entitlements. Google handles payment details; the App does not request or store your full bank card details. The App may access purchase status, product identifiers and purchase tokens needed to check whether premium features have been purchased.

In the current testing version, publisher-side purchase verification is not active and premium unlocking is unavailable. If we enable a verification service in a subsequent release, we may securely transmit a purchase token and app/device-bound entitlement information to our service to validate premium access. We will update this policy to identify any additional processing before enabling it.

4. Purposes of processing

Information is handled to provide local recordings, enable requested camera/microphone functions, administer advertising consent, display or measure ads, prevent fraud or misuse, check paid feature access, and maintain reliability and security.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Recorded media is stored on your device and remains there until you delete it or device storage management removes it. You may delete recordings through your device's Gallery, Files, or other media-management tools. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete media that was saved in shared device storage.

The App may store local settings and advertising preferences. Uninstalling the App generally removes its private application data, subject to Android behaviour. Google and other service providers control retention for data they receive under their own policies. We do not maintain a first-party account database or server-side copy of your recordings in the current test build.

6. Information security

The App relies on Android permissions and device security for access to camera, microphone, and locally stored media. Where network communication is used, Google SDKs and any configured purchase-verification services use encrypted connections. No application or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera and microphone permissions, stop capture, delete saved recordings, and manage device advertising controls. If your region grants rights to access, correct or erase personal information processed by us, contact us using the privacy email above. For personal information processed independently by Google, consult Google's privacy tools and policies.

8. Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 or the applicable age of digital consent in their country. It is intended for people able to understand the device permissions and recording responsibilities. We do not knowingly operate a first-party service to collect children's personal information.

9. Responsible use

Only record in ways permitted by applicable law and with any necessary consent from other people. The App is not intended to circumvent platform camera/microphone privacy indicators.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the App's features, service providers, advertising or payment implementation, or legal requirements change. We will publish an updated effective date on this page.
